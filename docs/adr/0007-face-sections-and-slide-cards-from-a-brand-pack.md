# Face sections and Slide cards drawn from a Brand pack

A Video is the approved Voiceover played under the Script's Face sections and Slide cards, and only the Face sections go through the face engine. A full talking head is expensive and drifts. The 77.6 s `script-01` render took 4,058 s in the face engine, and the head lurched and drifted after about 50 s. Three other layouts were weighed: cutaways from a full-length face, a split screen or face bubble over the card, and a choice of layout per card. Each needs the face rendered for the whole Voiceover, so they were rejected. The shape comes from the approved look's Shorts. Its starter Brand pack is kept in `config/brand-pack-starter/`.

The Script carries its cards after a `<!-- slides -->` marker. Each entry is triggered by a spoken phrase, not a time, so it survives a remade Voiceover. A `kind: face` entry brings the face back, and a Script whose first entry is at its opening words with no `kind: face` entry makes a cards-only Video, which needs no Likeness and no GPU. The `voiceover` command transcribes the finished Voiceover and saves its word timings next to it. Every phrase is matched against those timings before any GPU work. The match tries the first four words, then fewer, with heard digits spelled out. Each Face section is rendered from its own slice of the Voiceover, running 0.2 s past each edge and trimmed back. All the slices of one Video go through a single face-engine session, so the models load once. Each clip is kept and reused while its Likeness and audio slice are unchanged, so editing a card costs no GPU time, and the GPU is cleared (ADR 0003) only when a Face section needs rendering.

The approved look's constants are copied unchanged: a card or Face section cuts in 0.12 s before its phrase; anything that would hold under 0.6 s is dropped with a warning; each card pushes in by 3.5% over its hold, with hard cuts between cards. There are two more warnings: when a card's text needs longer to read than the card holds (15 characters a second plus 1.5 s), and when the text runs outside the frame. A Face section longer than 45 s gets a warning too, because of the drift. The Video runs to the end of the Voiceover (a Brand pack's end card, below, then adds 2.5 s); the Speech Check already trims extra words, so the approved look's tail cut is not used.

Cards are drawn by screenshotting a Brand pack's `slide.html` in headless Chrome, a local program, so ADR 0001 holds. A Brand pack belongs to the Persona, in `persona/<name>/brand-packs/<pack>/`, and a Persona can have several. `channel.yaml` names one with `brand_pack:`, which can be left out when the Persona has only one. One `slide.html` lays itself out for both 9:16 and 16:9. Loudness is below. Its constants are in `frame.py` rather than `pack.json`, because the starter pack has no loudness setting. The issue assumed one; that assumption is open for the Operator to decide.

The hook box came next, copied from the approved look's face-section code. The Script's front matter gives `hook` and `hook_hold`, and its `*words*` take the pack's `colors.teal`. The box is drawn at 1080×420 in white 76 px text with a 4 px black outline, its top 1150 px down a 1920 px frame (`HOOK_Y` 1300 less 150), and it shows until 0.05 s before `hook_hold` ends, never past the opening Face section. The approved look's default `hook_hold` is not in the kept files, so a Script with a hook must give one. The approved look made only 9:16, so in 16:9 the box is scaled by the frame's height, keeping its share of the frame; the Operator checks it by eye. Hook text is escaped, so it is drawn as written.

Captions and the wordmark badge were built over the Face sections and then removed at the Operator's request: neither is drawn. The Brand pack's `wordmark`, `fonts.caption_family` and `fonts.caption_file` settings are no longer read. The approved look's constants stay in `frame.py`.

The badge then came back at the Operator's request (2026-10-26), in a new place and look: the Brand pack's mark and `wordmark` in a 360×100 chip at (48, 1500) of the 1920 px frame, bottom left, below the hook and above the platforms' caption strip, over every Face section and never over a Slide card. The approved look's badge sat at (40, 1510) at 320×76; the new size is CloneCraft's own, so it lives in `video.py`, not `frame.py`. It is drawn only when the pack sets `wordmark`, so a pack without one is unchanged. Captions stay removed. The same request added an end card, a Brand pack setting `end_card` (`line`, `url`) drawn at the Format's size and held for 2.5 s in silence after the Voiceover. This is the one exception to the Video running to the end of the Voiceover: the audio is padded with silence, and the 0.4 s fade still ends with the speech. A closing Slide card was weighed and left out, because it would show while the Persona is still speaking and repeat the end card. A pack may also name a serif, `fonts.artifact`, for the cards' `*words*`, as the example.com site sets its emphasis.

The disclosure pill is the approved look's `pill` mode: the Brand pack's `disclosure` text drawn by the template's pill mode at the approved look's 720×84 box (`PILL_BOX`), overlaid centred at `PILL_Y` 1330 px down the frame and held over the whole last Slide card. It is always on when the pack has the setting. A Video in which no Slide card is drawn leaves it out. When the Brand pack was drawn on anyway (by a hook, or by a card later left out), a warning names the Script file.

Loudness is the approved look's `_loudnorm` pass: a first pass measures the Voiceover, and a second applies `loudnorm` to -14 LUFS with a true-peak target of -1.5 dBFS and `alimiter` at 0.794 linear (-2 dBFS). It is applied to the Video's audio only, so the approved Voiceover file is never changed. Silence cannot be normalised and stays silent. The approved look's QC then reads the finished Video with `ebur128`: integrated loudness outside -14 ±1.5 LUFS, or a true peak above -1.0 dBFS, is a warning and the Video is still written, as the approved look's compose step does for shortfalls; a stricter check would fail them instead. A take too short to measure (`ebur128` gates in 400 ms blocks, so a 0.3 s take reads -70 LUFS) passes unchanged and is warned about the same way. The approved look's 0.4 s fade-out at the end is copied: its `afade` sits in the same chain, and the decision was to copy the approved look's behaviour unchanged. The fade starts at 0 for a take shorter than 0.4 s. Loudness has no timing to place: it is set for the whole Video, so the timing rule does not apply.

The approved look's frame size, hook position, hook box, pill and loudness constants live once, in `frame.py`, and every other module reads them from there.
