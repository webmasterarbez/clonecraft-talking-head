# A Likeness per orientation, and Formats named by aspect

A YouTube Channel publishes both vertical Shorts and horizontal long-form Videos under one handle, so a Channel has one or more Formats, at most one per aspect, and the aspect (`9:16` or `16:9`) is the Format's name; no other aspect is supported. The Likeness files are 3:4 portraits, and a 16:9 centre crop of one keeps a strip about 42% of its height, which cuts off the head. Outpainting a landscape image would edit a real person's picture and need another local model (ADR 0001). So a Persona has at most one Likeness per orientation: `likeness.*` is the vertical one and `likeness-horizontal.*` the horizontal one, chosen by the Operator, and a Format with no matching Likeness stops the Video step. Both orientations share the face engine and models of ADR 0002, rendering at 480×832 or 832×480 and upscaling to the Format; the 832×480 size was checked on the 12 GB card with a test render (see the note below). One Voiceover per Script serves every Format.

Verified 2026-10-24 on the RTX 5070: an 8-second 16:9 render at 832×480 took 560.4 s of face-engine time and peaked at 11,426 MiB, and the Operator approved the clip. 16:9 is on.
